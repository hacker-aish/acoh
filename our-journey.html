WHERE IT STARTED

Every Great Project
Starts With a Plan.

ACOH was shaped by the idea of creating a simpler and more coordinated experience for people planning to design and build.

Architecture defines the vision of a project. Construction turns that vision into reality. When both teams work together from the beginning, communication becomes clearer and project coordination becomes easier.

That thinking became the foundation of Architect & Construction On Hire.

OUR EVOLUTION

How Our Journey Evolved.

From architecture-focused services to an integrated design and construction approach.

01
THE BEGINNING

Architecture On Hire

The journey began with a focus on making professional architectural services easier to access for residential and commercial projects.

  • 2D Floor Planning
  • Structural Design
  • 3D Elevation Design
  • Working Drawings
02
EXPANDING THE VISION

Beyond Basic Planning

As project requirements became more detailed, the service approach expanded beyond floor plans to support broader architectural coordination.

  • Electrical Layouts
  • Plumbing Layouts
  • MEP Planning
  • AR / VR Visualization
03
FROM DESIGN TO EXECUTION

Construction On Hire

The next stage was to connect good planning with professional execution, extending the approach into construction and project delivery.

  • Residential Construction
  • Commercial Construction
  • RCC Structure
  • Project Management
04
ONE INTEGRATED APPROACH

Architect And Construction On Hire

Architecture and construction came together under one integrated approach — creating ACOH as a single point for design, planning and project execution.

One Team. One Process. One Responsibility.

MOVING FORWARD

Our journey continues with a focus on coordinated design, transparent communication and quality project execution.
