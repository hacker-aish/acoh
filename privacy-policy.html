01
INTRODUCTION

About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Architect & Construction On Hire ("ACOH", "we", "us" or "our") handles information provided by users, customers, visitors and other individuals who interact with our website or services.

By accessing our website, submitting an enquiry or using our services, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law.

02
INFORMATION COLLECTION

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide and certain technical information generated when you use our website.

Name and contact information.
Phone number and email address.
Project location and project requirements.
Information submitted through website enquiry or consultation forms.
Communications you send to us.
Technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and website activity, where collected.
03
USE OF INFORMATION

How We May Use Your Information

We may use information for legitimate business purposes connected with our architectural, construction and related professional services.

  • Responding to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Understanding project requirements.
  • Preparing quotations, proposals or service information.
  • Communicating regarding an ongoing or potential project.
  • Providing architecture, construction or related services.
  • Improving our website, services and customer experience.
  • Maintaining business and operational records.
  • Protecting our website and systems from misuse, fraud or security threats.
  • Complying with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
04
INFORMATION SHARING

When Information May Be Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may, however, be shared with parties that assist us in operating our business or delivering services where reasonably necessary and permitted by law.

  • Employees and authorized team members.
  • Consultants, architects, engineers, contractors or project partners where necessary for a project.
  • Website hosting, cloud, communication or technology service providers.
  • Professional advisers such as legal or accounting professionals.
  • Government or regulatory authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, we expect them to handle it appropriately and in accordance with applicable obligations.

05
COOKIES & TECHNOLOGY

Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, understand website performance and improve the user experience.

Some analytics, advertising, embedded content or third-party services may also use their own technologies where those services are enabled on our website.

You can generally control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06
DATA SECURITY

How We Protect Information

We take reasonable administrative, organizational and technical measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure.

Access Control

Information access is intended to be limited to authorized persons where appropriate.

Security Measures

Reasonable measures may be used to protect systems and stored information.

Responsible Storage

Information is retained and managed according to operational and legal requirements.

No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07
DATA RETENTION

How Long We Keep Information

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including project administration, customer communication, record keeping, dispute resolution and compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the nature of the relationship and applicable legal requirements.

08
YOUR INFORMATION

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights or choices regarding personal information relating to you.

  • Request information about how your personal information is handled.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion or erasure where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, subject to applicable limitations.
  • Raise a concern regarding the handling of your information.

Requests may be subject to identity verification, legal requirements, contractual obligations and permitted exceptions.

09
EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites or integrate third-party services such as maps, social media, communication tools or analytics platforms.

Those third parties may operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review the privacy information provided by any third-party service they choose to use.

10
CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Children's Information

Our website and professional services are not primarily directed toward children. We do not intentionally seek to collect personal information from children except where appropriate, lawful and necessary.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been provided to us improperly, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11
POLICY UPDATES

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices or applicable requirements.

When this policy is updated, the revised version may be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
